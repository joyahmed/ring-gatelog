# GateLog voice-over script (for Joy)

Video: `../ring-gatelog-video/gatelog-v1.mp4` (1:45). Play it muted while you read, or just read at a calm pace.
Each block has a time window. If you go long, that is fine: I will stretch the scene to fit your voice.

**How to record**
- Phone voice memo is enough. Quiet room, phone 20 cm from your mouth, no music.
- Read each block, pause 2 seconds, go to the next. Mistake? Pause, re-read the whole block. I cut the bad take.
- Send me one file (m4a, mp3 or wav). Put it in `~/projects/hackathon/ring-gatelog-video/voice.m4a` or drop it in chat.

**[0:00 - 0:08] Title**
> Every housing society has a gate. But the committee never really knows who came in, when, or if the gate was left open. GateLog fixes that.

**[0:08 - 0:22] Real Ring API**
> GateLog talks to the Ring API from the server. This is a real response from the Ring sandbox: one device, the Playground Device. And here are fourteen real events from its history, on the twenty-ninth of September.

**[0:22 - 0:28] Honest note**
> We recorded these responses once with a Playground token, so the demo runs the same way every time.

**[0:28 - 0:43] How events arrive**
> In real life, Ring pushes every event to our webhook: motion, a person, a vehicle, the doorbell, the gate sensor. Every message is signed, and we check the signature. The sandbox has no gate traffic, so we play a scripted gate day through the same webhook, and the page clearly says "Demo data".

**[0:43 - 0:57] The verdict**
> Here is that day. The report starts with the answer. One incident: the gate was left open for twenty-five minutes, from three ten to three thirty-five. Below that, the whole day in six numbers.

**[0:57 - 1:13] The details**
> Hour by hour, you see the busy morning and evening, the night hours in grey, and when the gate stood open. Flagged items come first. Then visitors: a person was seen, and rang the bell within two minutes. And every single event, oldest first.

**[1:13 - 1:29] Phone and dark mode**
> It works on a phone, for the guard or the secretary. And it follows dark mode.

**[1:29 - 1:35] Print**
> And it prints on one page, ready for the committee meeting.

**[1:35 - 1:45] Close**
> GateLog only reads events. It never opens or locks the gate. Ring events in, a clear daily report out. Thank you.
